I am a postdoctoral researcher at the [INRIA Center](https://www.inria.fr/en) of the University of Lille. My research fields are applied mathematics, operations research and energy economics. Check my ongoing research projects and working papers in the page "Publications".

I hold a PhD in Mathematical Engineering from the [Center for Operations Research and Econometrics (CORE/LIDAM)](https://uclouvain.be/en/research-institutes/lidam/core), UCLouvain, obtained in December 2024 under the supervision of Prof. [Anthony Papavasiliou](https://ap-rg.eu/).
Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher with the [Max Planck Institute for Research on Collective Goods](https://www.econ.mpg.de/), doing research under the supervision of Prof. [Peter Cramton](https://cramton.umd.edu/). I was also a visiting researcher at the [Harvard Kennedy School](https://www.hks.harvard.edu/) working with Prof. [William Hogan](https://whogan.scholars.harvard.edu/).
